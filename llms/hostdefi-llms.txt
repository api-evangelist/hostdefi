# HostDeFi

> Non-custodial trading app with a built-in safety layer: a free token-safety
> scanner that grades any token from A+ to F using on-chain checks (mint and
> freeze authority, liquidity depth, holder concentration, contract flags),
> plus swaps on Solana (Jupiter-routed) and major EVM chains (KyberSwap-routed).
> No signup, no custody, no HostDeFi token. The scanner is free (fair use:
> 100 checks/day per IP); swaps carry a disclosed 3% platform fee.

## Core
- [HostDeFi app](https://hostdefi.com/): swaps on Solana and the major EVM chains, wallet-native, no KYC
- [Token safety scanner](https://hostdefi.com/scan): paste any contract address or ticker for a free A+ to F on-chain risk grade
- [Guides library](https://hostdefi.com/guides/): plain-English token-safety guides (every guide listed below)
- [Open data](https://hostdefi.com/data/): weekly token safety snapshot - free, citable CSV, updated Mondays
- [Free API docs](https://hostdefi.com/docs/api/): free tier (100 checks/day per IP), keys, and the x402 machine-payable lane
- [Sitemap](https://hostdefi.com/sitemap.xml)
